Detector bands

SPHEREx has six detectors. Each one sees a different slice of 0.75 to 5 µm, and the wavelength changes across the array, so the same sky position lands at a different colour in every exposure.

Data: SPHEREx Level 2 spectral images (IMAGE extension, MJy/sr) from NASA/IPAC IRSA, fetched as cutouts around the target's epoch-corrected position. Each cutout is resampled onto a shared north-up grid of 3.075″ pixels by bilinear interpolation. Gray pixels have no data or were masked by the pipeline. Predicted position uses published proper motion for the target. Flux is the background-subtracted sum inside a 2.5-pixel circle.
